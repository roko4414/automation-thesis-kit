# Workflow automation thesis — scoping package

For: Nevena Marić, MSc thesis, University of St. Gallen
Supervisor brief: Roger Moser, 31 March — build a live workflow automation on n8n,
wrap it (input form / output visualisation / productisation), and write the thesis as
documentation and justification of that build.

## What this package is

A clean-room scaffold. Industry, architecture, method and evaluation protocol are
specified; **you build it and you write it**. That is deliberate — the brief says the
point is that *you* acquire the skills, and it is your name on the submission. What
you get here is the scoping and review that a supervisor cannot give you at this
level of detail, not the artefact itself.

## What this package is NOT

No APG client material. No client-derived workflows, figures or documents. The
reference business in `02-system-architecture.md` is a composite constructed from
publicly observable characteristics of job-based trade businesses, not from any
engagement. This matters for you too: a thesis you can publish, show in interviews
and defend without confidentiality caveats is worth more than one you cannot.

## Read in this order

1. `01-methodology.md` — the discovery → quantify → bound → build → measure method.
   This is your methods chapter.
2. `02-system-architecture.md` — the system to build, and the design argument behind it.
3. `03-measurement-design.md` — how to produce defensible before/after results.
4. `04-build-plan.md` — sequencing, and where to stop.

## The one decision to make first

Pick your reference business and get access to it. Everything else depends on it.
Options, in descending order of thesis quality:

- **A real small trade business that will let you observe and instrument it.** Best
  possible outcome: real baseline data, real results chapter. One owner-operator with
  20–60 jobs a month is enough. This is worth two weeks of cold outreach.
- **A real business you can observe but not instrument.** You get a genuine process
  map and genuine time estimates from observation; your results become a simulation
  validated against observed inputs. Still a strong thesis, stated honestly.
- **A fully constructed reference case.** Weakest, but viable if you are explicit that
  the contribution is the design and the method, not the measured effect.

Do not slide from option 1 to option 3 silently partway through. Whichever you are on,
the thesis says so in the methods chapter.
